============================================================
 DLoc THESIS — PROSE REWRITE PACK
============================================================

This folder contains the full prose of the thesis, extracted from the LaTeX
chapters and split into 5 batches of ~2,000 words for you and your friend to
rewrite in your own voice.

FILES
  batch1.txt  Abstract + Chapter 1 (Introduction) + Chapter 2 (2.1, 2.2)
  batch2.txt  Chapter 2 (2.3, 2.4) + Chapter 3 (intro, 3.1-3.3)
  batch3.txt  Chapter 3 (3.4-3.6) + Chapter 4 (Implementation) + Chapter 5 (5.1)
  batch4.txt  Chapter 5 (5.2, 5.3, 5.4)
  batch5.txt  Chapter 5 (5.5-5.7) + Chapter 6 + Chapter 7 + Appendix prose

HOW TO USE
  1. Rewrite every paragraph in your own voice. Split the batches between the
     two of you however you like.
  2. KEEP the section headings (e.g. "1.1 Background and Motivation") and the
     [cite: X] markers exactly where they are. They tell me where each piece
     goes and which reference to restore.
  3. IGNORE tables, equations, and figures - they are NOT in these files because
     they stay exactly as built in the .tex chapters. Where the prose mentions
     "Table 5.1", "Figure 5.2", or "Equation 3.1", just keep that as plain text.
  4. Hand each edited file back and I will (a) drop your text into the matching
     chapters/*.tex file and (b) remove any clunky AI-tell phrasing without
     changing your meaning.
